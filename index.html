Aether Account & Data Deletion

Permanent deletion request portal for Aether users.

Option 1: In-App Deletion Instant & Recommended

If you have the Aether app installed on your phone or desktop, you can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data immediately without waiting:

  1. Open the Aether app.
  2. Tap on Settings from the main menu.
  3. Scroll down to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Data.
  5. Complete identity re-verification (Google Sign-In or Password).
  6. Confirm deletion — all your notes, attendance records, study logs, and tasks will be erased instantly.
